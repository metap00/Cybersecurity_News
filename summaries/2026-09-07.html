💡 Today I Learned: BGP Hijacking ▶
BGP (Border Gateway Protocol) is the routing system that decides how data travels across the internet between large networks called Autonomous Systems — essentially the postal service for internet traffic. BGP hijacking happens when a malicious or misconfigured network falsely announces that it owns a block of IP addresses, causing internet traffic destined for the real owner to be rerouted through the attacker's infrastructure instead. This has been used to intercept cryptocurrency wallets, eavesdrop on email, and redirect traffic from major cloud providers, making it one of the most impactful infrastructure-level attacks possible.
📖 Glossary: BGP · AS · RPKI ▶
BGP — Border Gateway Protocol; the routing protocol that exchanges reachability information between Autonomous Systems and determines internet-wide traffic paths.
AS — Autonomous System; a collection of IP networks under a single administrative entity (e.g., an ISP or large enterprise) that shares a common routing policy on the internet.
RPKI — Resource Public Key Infrastructure; a cryptographic framework that lets IP address holders digitally sign their BGP route announcements, helping prevent BGP hijacking by validating route origins.

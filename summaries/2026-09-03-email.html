💡 Today I Learned: Canary Token
A canary token is a digital tripwire — a unique, trackable link, file, or credential embedded in a system that silently alerts defenders the moment an attacker accesses it. Security teams plant them across environments to catch intruders who've already breached the perimeter before any real damage is done.

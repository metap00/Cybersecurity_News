💡 Today I Learned: BGP Hijacking
BGP (Border Gateway Protocol) is the routing system that decides how data travels across the internet between large networks. BGP hijacking occurs when an attacker falsely announces ownership of IP address blocks, rerouting traffic through their infrastructure to intercept, spy on, or disrupt communications.

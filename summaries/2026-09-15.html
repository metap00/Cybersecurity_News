💡 Today I Learned: Shadow IT ▶
Shadow IT refers to software, devices, or cloud services that employees use for work without explicit approval or knowledge of the IT or security team. While it often starts with good intentions — employees trying to boost productivity — these unauthorized tools bypass security controls like data loss prevention, access auditing, and patch management, creating invisible attack surfaces that defenders don't know to monitor. Security teams use automated discovery tools and cloud access security brokers (CASBs) to detect Shadow IT and bring those assets under governed policy before attackers exploit them first.
📖 Glossary: Botnet · RaaS · SOAR · XDR · Threat Hunting ▶
Botnet — A network of compromised computers remotely controlled by an attacker to carry out coordinated malicious activities such as DDoS attacks, spam campaigns, or credential stuffing.
RaaS (Ransomware-as-a-Service) — A criminal business model where ransomware developers lease their malware and infrastructure to affiliate attackers in exchange for a share of ransom payments collected.
SOAR (Security Orchestration, Automation and Response) — A platform that integrates security tools, automates repetitive analyst tasks, and manages incident response playbooks to help security teams respond to threats faster.
XDR (Extended Detection and Response) — A security approach that collects and correlates threat telemetry across endpoint, network, email, and cloud layers to provide unified detection and coordinated response capabilities.
Threat Hunting — A proactive security practice where analysts actively search through networks and systems for hidden threats or indicators of compromise that automated detection tools may have missed.

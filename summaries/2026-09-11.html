💡 Today I Learned: Supply Chain Attack ▶
A supply chain attack targets the tools, libraries, or services that software depends on rather than targeting the software itself directly. By compromising a trusted upstream component — such as a build tool, open-source package, or vendor software update mechanism — attackers can silently distribute malicious code to thousands of downstream organizations in a single move. The 2020 SolarWinds breach and the 2024 XZ Utils backdoor are landmark examples showing how one compromised upstream component can give nation-state actors access to hundreds of critical targets simultaneously.
📖 Glossary: Lateral Movement · Patch Tuesday · Red Team ▶
Lateral Movement — The techniques an attacker uses to progressively move through a network after initial access, seeking higher-value systems or credentials.
Patch Tuesday — Microsoft's informal name for the second Tuesday of each month, when the company releases a batch of security updates for its software products.
Red Team — A group of security professionals authorized to simulate real adversary tactics against an organization to identify weaknesses before actual attackers do.

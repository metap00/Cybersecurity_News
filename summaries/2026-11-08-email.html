💡 Today I Learned: Certificate Transparency
Certificate Transparency requires every TLS certificate to be publicly recorded in append-only, cryptographically verifiable CT Logs before browsers will trust it. If a certificate authority is compromised and issues a fraudulent certificate for your domain, you can detect it within hours rather than waiting months for the fraud to surface.

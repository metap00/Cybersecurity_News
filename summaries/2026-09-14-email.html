💡 Today I Learned: Passkey
A passkey replaces passwords with a device-held private key that never leaves your hardware — your login proves possession by signing a challenge, eliminating phishable shared secrets. Built on FIDO2/WebAuthn, passkeys are already supported across all major platforms.

💡 Today I Learned: Supply Chain Attack
A supply chain attack compromises an upstream component — a build tool, open-source library, or vendor update — to silently distribute malicious code to thousands of downstream organizations. Examples include the 2020 SolarWinds breach and the 2024 XZ Utils backdoor.

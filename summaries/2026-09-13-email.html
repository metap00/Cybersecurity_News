💡 Today I Learned: Fuzzing
Fuzzing is a technique where automated tools feed random or malformed inputs into software to discover crashes and security vulnerabilities — many critical CVEs in browsers and OS kernels have been found this way. Tools like AFL++ and libFuzzer make fuzzing accessible to both professional researchers and everyday developers.
